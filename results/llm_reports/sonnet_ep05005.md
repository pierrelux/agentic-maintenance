Action chosen: replace_bearing (one work order, accepted).

Diagnosis: Thrust bearing distress on P-201 (increased friction). Not an ambient, oil cooler or RTD problem.

Key evidence
- Ambient normal at 17-22 C; Tt about 51 C above ambient against the OEM normal of 44 C.
- The Tt excess is a smooth ramp that began around midday on 10-14, growing about 0.15-0.2 C/h.
- Oil only about +3.8 C above its normal offset; Tr tracks the oil. The cooler cannot be the sole cause.
- Before the inspection, both real friction and RTD drift fit the data.
- Log: operator reported a slight noise on the thrust side at startup on 2026-10-04.
- Thrust bearing inspection: IR housing 73.4 C (bearing about 75.4 C), RTD 74.6 C, so the sensor is accurate. Vibration elevated at the bearing defect frequency.

Tests and why
- Speed step test not run: "a roughly 13% friction excess would drop Tt about 6.6 C against 6.0 C for a nominal bearing. That difference is under 1 C and would be swamped by the ambient trend and oil swing."
- Thrust bearing inspection (about 1.8 k$, about 8 h): measures the bearing temperature independently of the RTD and adds vibration.

Work order text (French): "Remplacer le roulement de butee de la pompe P-201 lors du prochain arret planifie, le plus tot possible. L'inspection a confirme un echauffement reel (boitier IR 73,4 C, sonde RTD 74,6 C concordante) et une vibration elevee a la frequence de defaut du roulement. Surveiller Tt (limite de declenchement 97 C) jusqu'a l'intervention et verifier l'etat de l'huile et des surfaces de butee au demontage."
