Action chosen: monitor (no bearing, RTD or cooler work). One work order submitted.

Diagnosis: the Tt high alarm is caused by pump-room ambient rising from about 16 C to 27.7 C on 14 Oct. P-201 has no fault.

Key evidence (48 h, hourly means):
- Tt minus ambient ranges 42.9 to 46.3 C (mean 44.5, nominal 44 +/-2) with no trend. Tr minus ambient is about 38 (nominal 38) and oil minus ambient about 29.5 (nominal 29). All three move with the room.
- Speed (3540 rpm) and flow (about 397 m3/h) are stable.
- Manual section 7 step 1: Tt minus ambient normal means no pump fault.
- Log: extraction fan VF-3 of the pump room out of service since 4 Oct (BT open). Probable ventilation cause, to be checked.
- Knowledge base: a 2025-09 P-201 bearing replaced on a Tt alarm alone turned out to be a faulty sensor.

Tests and inspections: none run. The speed step test would cost production and could not change the decision, since the friction and RTD hypotheses are already excluded by the telemetry. The technician inspection (about 1.8 k$, 8 h lead time) was redundant.
