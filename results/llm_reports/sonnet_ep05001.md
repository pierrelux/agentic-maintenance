Action chosen: clean_cooler (work order submitted and accepted).

Diagnosis: The water side of P-201's oil cooler is fouled. The thrust bearing high-temperature alarm (73.0 C against a 72.0 C limit) is a consequence of the oil overheating.

Key evidence
- Room normal (18 C); Tt minus room only about 5 C above nominal, ruling out ambient.
- Oil surge: from about 06:30 on 10-16, the oil rose from about 46 C to 55 C in 4 h and was still accelerating; the room rose only about 2.9 C.
- Net of the room effect, Tr followed the oil roughly one for one and Tt followed at about 0.5 C per C of oil (manual: about 0.45), the fouling signature.
- Not bearing friction: a friction fault raises the oil only about 0.2 C per C of Tt; here the oil rose about twice as much as Tt.
- Technician: the oil cooler inspection found "significant fouling on water side".
- Unexplained Tt drift: at equal oil and room conditions, Tt has crept up about 3 C/day since 10-14, roughly +5 C, either RTD drift or early friction growth, not resolved. The agent asked for an RTD check if Tt minus room stays above about 48 C once the oil is back to normal.

Tests and inspections
- Oil cooler inspection (1.8 k$, about 8 h): the cooler was the leading cause and the most time-critical.
- No speed step test: "the extra drop from friction would be only about 0.6 C. The oil ramp running at about 2.6 C/h would swamp that."

Work order text (French): "Nettoyer le refroidisseur d'huile de la pompe P-201 côté eau (encrassement important constaté à l'inspection), puis vérifier le débit d'eau de refroidissement. Après le nettoyage, confirmer que la température d'huile revient à environ ambiance + 29 °C et que l'alarme Tt est levée. Si Tt reste supérieure d'environ 48 °C à l'ambiance avec une huile normale, faire un contrôle de la boucle RTD de la butée."
